Title: Rest In Peace the Robot Uprising obstacle course
Author: Robot Uprising Crew
Hashtags: competition, track

The original Robot Uprising obstacle course track has been retired and today met it's lore appropriate end at the hands of a giantic robot crusher (Sortti Station Kivikko). Past it's prime in our bigger hackathons (2017—2019) parts of the obstacle course returned as props and challenges for Disobey, but now it was time to make space for new things.
